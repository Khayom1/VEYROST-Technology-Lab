Transparency and Accountability Policy

1. Our Commitment

VEYROST Technology Lab recognizes that public trust must be earned through accurate information, responsible financial management, and verifiable progress.

This document establishes the principles that will guide project reporting and the handling of voluntary contributions.

2. Financial Transparency

The project intends to maintain public records of:

- Initial funding objectives
- Confirmed contributions
- Payment-processing fees
- Net contributions received
- Project-related expenditures
- Equipment purchases
- Remaining project funds, where they can be reliably established

3. Appropriate Use of Funds

Project funds are intended exclusively for legitimate activities connected to the documented objectives of VEYROST Technology Lab.

They must not knowingly be used for:

- Gambling or betting
- Personal entertainment
- Unrelated personal purchases
- Cryptocurrency speculation
- Unrelated investments
- Unapproved private expenses

4. Documentation

Where reasonably available and legally shareable, financial records may include:

- Receipts
- Invoices
- Order confirmations
- Transaction records
- Equipment photographs
- Project progress reports

Sensitive personal information, payment credentials, and confidential details must be removed before publication.

5. Accurate Reporting

The project will distinguish clearly between:

- Planned activities and completed activities
- Estimated budgets and actual costs
- Intended funding and received contributions
- Aspirational milestones and achieved results

No equipment, prototype, partnership, or achievement will be presented as real unless it has actually been obtained or completed.

6. Financial Corrections

If a published financial record contains an error, the correction should be documented.

Material changes to budgets and allocations should include an explanation.

7. Limitations

This repository is a public project documentation space. It is not, by itself, an independent audit or a guarantee of financial performance.

The initiative does not claim registered charity status, tax-exempt status, or independent audit certification unless such status is formally established and documented.

8. Public Accountability

Community members may submit questions, suggestions, and requests for clarification through GitHub Issues.

Constructive questions about project objectives, spending, and progress are welcome.

---

Our principle: Every public claim should be accurate, and every reported expense should have a clear purpose.
