
VEYROST Technology Lab — Development Roadmap

Overview

This roadmap describes the intended development of VEYROST Technology Lab, from initial preparation to a more capable independent technology laboratory.

All milestones are objectives. Their completion depends on available resources, funding, technical feasibility, and actual progress.

Phase 0 — Project Preparation

Status: In progress

Objectives:

- Establish the public GitHub repository
- Publish the project mission and vision
- Document preliminary financial plans
- Establish transparent reporting principles
- Investigate legitimate funding and payment options
- Identify initial equipment requirements

Expected outcome:

A documented and publicly accessible project foundation.

Phase 1 — Initial Foundation

Funding milestone: US$10,000

Status: Planned

Objectives:

- Acquire essential computing hardware
- Obtain initial 3D printing capabilities
- Purchase electronics and microcontroller components
- Establish a basic prototyping workspace
- Acquire essential workshop tools and safety equipment

Expected outcome:

The ability to begin practical experiments and build initial prototypes.

Phase 2 — Capability Expansion

Aspirational funding milestone: US$25,000–US$50,000

Status: Long-term objective

Objectives:

- Expand equipment capacity
- Improve prototyping capabilities
- Develop more complex electronic systems
- Expand mechanical design and manufacturing resources
- Support a wider range of experimental projects

Expected outcome:

A more capable engineering and prototyping environment.

Phase 3 — Advanced Development

Aspirational funding milestone: US$100,000+

Status: Long-term objective

Objectives:

- Develop advanced robotics prototypes
- Explore automation systems
- Improve testing and measurement capabilities
- Build more sophisticated experimental devices
- Support larger engineering projects

Expected outcome:

The ability to work on more advanced independent technology projects.

Phase 4 — Long-Term Laboratory Vision

Aspirational funding milestone: US$1,000,000+

Status: Long-term vision

Objectives:

- Develop a fully equipped independent laboratory
- Expand engineering and research capabilities
- Establish sustainable project operations
- Support long-term product development
- Explore collaboration with educational and technical communities

Expected outcome:

A mature technology development environment with broader research and engineering capabilities.

Progress Reporting

A milestone will only be marked as completed when supported by actual evidence.

Evidence may include:

- Equipment acquisition records
- Photographs
- Technical demonstrations
- Prototype documentation
- Published project reports

Roadmap Changes

The roadmap may be revised when technical requirements, available resources, or project priorities change.

Material changes will be documented publicly.
