
Donations and Voluntary Contributions

1. Purpose

This document explains the principles governing voluntary financial contributions to VEYROST Technology Lab.

The initiative aims to establish an independent technology prototyping environment through transparent, voluntary support.

2. Voluntary Nature of Contributions

All contributions are voluntary.

A contribution does not provide the donor with:

- Ownership or equity in the project
- Repayment rights
- Interest or financial returns
- Guaranteed products or services
- Control over project operations

Contributions are intended to support the stated technology development objectives.

3. Use of Contributions

Contributions may support legitimate project expenses, including:

- Computing equipment
- 3D printing equipment
- Electronics and robotics components
- Workshop tools
- Prototyping materials
- Shipping and customs costs
- Essential technical infrastructure

The proposed initial allocation is available in "BUDGET.md" (BUDGET.md).

4. Payment Methods

Donation status: Not yet officially open.

Payment methods will only be published after their availability, ownership, verification requirements, fees, and compliance conditions have been confirmed.

No payment should be sent to an unverified account or address claiming to represent this project.

When verified payment methods become available, this section will be updated with:

- Payment provider
- Supported currency
- Applicable network, if relevant
- Fees or conversion considerations
- Instructions for verification

Donor names and personal details will not be published without explicit permission.

6. Fees and Net Contributions

Where applicable, payment-processing fees, conversion fees, and other transaction costs will be recorded separately.

Reports will distinguish between:

- Gross contributions
- Processing fees
- Net funds received

7. Refunds and Reversals

Contributions are voluntary and are not investments or loans.

Any refund, reversal, or dispute will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the relevant payment provider's rules.

8. No Misrepresentation

VEYROST Technology Lab will not:

- Guarantee fundraising success
- Promise financial returns
- Claim charity or tax-exempt status without legal basis
- Publish unverified payment details
- Misrepresent planned equipment as already purchased
- Present estimated contributions as confirmed funds

9. Updates

This document will be updated when verified contribution methods become available and when confirmed contributions are received.
