VEYROST Technology Lab — Expense Register

1. Purpose

This document provides a public record of expenditures associated with VEYROST Technology Lab.

Its purpose is to make project-related spending traceable and understandable.

2. Expense Reporting Principles

Every recorded expense should include, where available:

- Transaction date
- Item or service purchased
- Supplier or merchant
- Amount and currency
- Project purpose
- Payment or transaction reference
- Supporting receipt or invoice

Personal and sensitive information will be redacted before publication.

4. Expense Categories

Expenses may be classified under:

1. Computing equipment
2. 3D printing equipment
3. Electronics and robotics
4. Workshop tools and safety
5. Prototyping materials
6. Software and technical infrastructure
7. Shipping, customs, and setup
8. Other approved project-related costs

5. Evidence and Verification

Supporting evidence may include:

- Receipts
- Invoices
- Order confirmations
- Payment confirmations
- Photographs of purchased equipment

Evidence will be published only when it can be shared safely and lawfully.

6. Corrections

If an error is discovered, the record will be corrected and the change documented.

Historical information should not be silently altered in a way that misrepresents previous reporting.

7. Important Note

A planned purchase is not an actual expense.

Only completed and reasonably verifiable transactions will be entered into the expense register.
