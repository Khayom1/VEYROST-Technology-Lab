VEYROST Technology Lab — Initial Budget

Initial Funding Objective: US$10,000

1. Purpose

This document outlines the preliminary allocation of the initial funding objective for VEYROST Technology Lab.

The proposed budget is designed to establish the basic equipment and resources required to begin practical technology prototyping.

All figures are planning estimates, not supplier quotations or completed purchases.

2. Proposed Budget Allocation

Category| Estimated Allocation
Computing workstation and hardware| $2,500
3D printer and accessories| $1,200
Electronics and robotics components| $1,000
Workshop tools and safety equipment| $1,000
Prototyping materials| $800
Software, storage, and connectivity| $500
Shipping, customs, and setup| $1,000
Contingency reserve| $2,000
Total| $10,000

3. Budget Details

Computing Workstation — $2,500

Planned resources:

- Development computer
- Processing and graphics capabilities suitable for engineering tasks
- Storage and backup devices
- Essential peripherals

3D Printing — $1,200

Planned resources:

- 3D printer
- Printing accessories
- Replacement parts
- Initial printing materials

Electronics and Robotics — $1,000

Planned resources:

- Microcontrollers
- Sensors
- Motors and drivers
- Circuit boards
- Electronic components
- Power supplies and testing accessories

Workshop Tools and Safety — $1,000

Planned resources:

- Hand tools
- Measuring instruments
- Soldering equipment
- Work surfaces
- Personal protective equipment
- Basic safety resources

Prototyping Materials — $800

Planned resources:

- Filaments and other printing materials
- Wires and connectors
- Mechanical components
- Enclosures and construction materials

Software, Storage, and Connectivity — $500

Planned resources:

- Essential software services where necessary
- Data storage
- Backup resources
- Internet and technical connectivity

Shipping, Customs, and Setup — $1,000

A preliminary allowance for transportation, delivery, customs-related costs where applicable, and equipment setup.

Contingency Reserve — $2,000

A reserve for unforeseen costs, price changes, replacement components, and essential project requirements.

Use of this reserve will be documented.

4. Budget Principles

1. Purchases must support the stated objectives of the project.
2. Actual prices must be recorded.
3. Significant deviations from the proposed allocation must be explained publicly.
4. Available receipts and invoices should be published with sensitive personal information removed.
5. Unused funds remain allocated to legitimate future project needs unless otherwise disclosed.

5. Budget Status

Status: Preliminary proposal — not yet spent.

No expenditure should be interpreted as completed merely because it appears in this document.

The budget will be updated when reliable supplier quotations and actual purchasing information become available.

6. Future Revisions

This budget may change because of:

- Market prices
- Product availability
- Shipping and customs costs
- Technical requirements
- Equipment compatibility

Any material revision will be recorded in the repository.
