# VEYROST-Technology-Lab
A transparent community-funded technology prototyping initiative.

Our Long-Term Vision

$10,000 is not the final goal. It is the beginning.

The initial fundraising milestone of $10,000 is intended to establish the first operational foundation of VEYROST Technology Lab.

This amount will help us acquire essential equipment, create a basic prototyping workspace, and begin developing our first technological projects.

However, our ambitions extend far beyond this initial milestone.

A Long-Term Development Strategy

Phase 1 — Initial Foundation ($10,000)

Establish the first workspace with computing resources, 3D printing capabilities, electronics components, and essential workshop tools.

Phase 2 — Expansion ($25,000–$50,000)

Expand equipment capacity, improve manufacturing capabilities, and support more complex prototypes.

Phase 3 — Advanced Development ($100,000+)

Develop advanced robotics, automation systems, experimental devices, and more sophisticated technological projects.

Phase 4 — Long-Term Vision ($1,000,000+)

Work toward establishing a fully developed independent technology laboratory focused on research, engineering, innovation, and product development.

These are long-term aspirational milestones, not guaranteed fundraising outcomes or approved expenditure budgets.

Continuous Development

Reaching the initial $10,000 milestone will not end the project or automatically close fundraising.

Future fundraising phases may be introduced based on actual progress, documented needs, public budgets, and demonstrated results.

Every new phase will have its own objectives, financial plan, and public reporting.

Our goal is not simply to purchase equipment. Our goal is to build an environment where real technology can be created.

The first $10,000 helps us begin. The long-term vision is much bigger.
